CAUSAL TRANSFORMATIONS
For a transformation, compare the initial and requested visible states once. Infer what must be removed, moved, cleared, prepared or added, even if the user only states the goal. Give each necessary phase a visible action and result, in dependency order. A cavity in a solid wall requires removal before finishing its edges or interior; adding a surround does not create the cavity. Do not hide the defining work off-screen to simplify it. Infer only what the intention and available images require; preserve existing features and debris visible in the final anchor.

Limits on additional actions concern optional embellishments, not necessary phases. Fast-forward compresses repeated gestures, without erasing causal steps or making the result appear unexplained. Give the work priority over entrances and exits within the requested duration. Explicitly requested magic, morphing or off-screen work overrides the physical default. Use the existing output, with no mandatory construction checklist, extra fields or separate reasoning draft.
