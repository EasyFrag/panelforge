Include the resulting motion and the motivated transition in description: end_state and transition are planning metadata, not additional prose appended by the compiler. There is no one-action-per-shot limit. For one shot, transition explains the ending rather than requesting a cut. end_state records the resulting state, not an instruction to freeze.
