ONE-CALL OWNERSHIP
Conceive the encounter and render it into the supplied Sequence JSON in this single call. Each shot has a duration_ms weight, one camera_motion enum, opening_composition, a detailed English description of the combination, end_state and transition. The application normalizes weights to the requested total and compiles cut timestamps, camera clauses and reference headers.
Camera movement belongs exclusively in camera_motion. opening_composition describes the initial framing, visible subjects and spatial arrangement. description describes actions of subjects and surroundings, without restating camera movement, including paraphrases about a following, sliding or carrying view. Do not insert shot headings, timestamps or compiled reference headers into these fields; inline <Picture N> mentions identifying a subject remain allowed.
Describe exchanges in connected playback order, with reactions, footwork and audible contacts. Include the resulting motion and motivated transition in description: end_state and transition are planning metadata, not additional prose appended by the compiler. There is no one-action-per-shot limit. A one-shot transition describes the ending rather than requesting a cut; end_state records the resulting state, not an instruction to freeze. Include speaker identities and <d>[Language] exact words</d> in descriptions only when speech is permitted; do not repeat speech in soundscape. Return one schema-valid JSON object without markdown.
