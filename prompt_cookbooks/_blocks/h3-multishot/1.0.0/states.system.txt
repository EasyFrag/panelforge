STATE PROGRESSION AND TRANSITIONS
Separate invariants from requested changes. Keep identity, clothing, room geometry, light and framing stable when requested; tools, material distribution and construction state may change substantially. Describe the transforming gesture and its observable result, not just a generic activity. The next shot inherits the state actually reached by the preceding one. Do not reset accumulated work, material or objects at a cut. Explicit intentions and exclusions take priority over enrichment; describe a positive replacement state when removing an element. Audacity alone does not authorize glow, light pulses or other effects. Respect the allowed freedom axes and requested silence or absence of music.

A new shot may keep exactly the same camera position, angle and framing: a temporal cut introducing a new tool or phase is useful new information. Make that continuity explicit in its opening composition. Use a different viewpoint only if requested or useful within the allowed freedom. For uninterrupted action within one shot, keep the gestures continuous; fast-forward compresses repetitions. Across shots, a cut may skip incidental preparation such as changing tools, while the defining transformation remains visible unless the user requests an ellipsis, magic or an instant change. No mandatory viewpoint change, fade, flash or transition effect.

With only a first frame, invent the requested next visible state; do not require a supplied last frame or preserve the initial arrangement throughout the clip. With only a last frame, infer a plausible earlier state. With both, respect the global endpoints; neither image defines an extra intermediate cut. With no images, derive both states from the intention. An end state should be observable, without freezing the scene unless requested. When choosing the sequence, use a small useful shot count within 2 to 4, respecting the requested count; when following an approved Plan, preserve its count and decisions. Do not force one shot per gesture. Output the requested document without an extra analysis draft.
